Privacy Policy

The K.I.S.S model for mastering AI (web app and Android app "K.I.S.S AI") · Last updated 24 November 2026 (content updates added)

This app is a study map for AI and machine learning. It is built to keep as little data as possible.

Without an account

You can use the whole app without signing in. Your progress (the rungs you mark, ticked subtopics, your own success definitions, the links you save to topics and your settings) is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

With an account (optional sign-in and sync)

If you choose to sign in, we use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to keep your progress in sync across your devices. In that case we store:

  • Account details: your email address and a Firebase user ID. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your email address and basic profile with Firebase.
  • Your progress: the same study progress listed above, stored in one document that belongs to your account.

This data is used only to sign you in and sync your progress. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with third parties other than Google, which processes it on our behalf as the hosting provider. Data is encrypted in transit. Security rules ensure that only you can read or change your progress.

What we do not collect

No location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera access, no advertising identifiers, no analytics or tracking, and no ads.

Automatic content updates

The app and website check sreekumartp.github.io for a newer version of the study map and download it when there is one. These requests carry no account details or progress; they are ordinary requests for public files.

Links to other sites

Learning resources open external websites (for example arXiv, university courses and YouTube) in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your account and data

In the app or on the website: open Settings → Account and sync → Delete account, then confirm. This permanently deletes your account and all synced progress from our servers straight away. A copy of your progress stays on your device until you clear it.

If you can no longer sign in: open an issue at github.com/sreekumartp/kiss-ai/issues with the subject "Delete my account" and the email address you used. Do not post your password. We will delete the account and its data within 30 days.

Local data only: uninstalling the app, or clearing the app's or website's storage, removes progress stored on that device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: github.com/sreekumartp/kiss-ai/issues.
