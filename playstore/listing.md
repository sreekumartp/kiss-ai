## Full description (max 4000 characters)
The K.I.S.S model for mastering AI turns the whole field of AI and machine learning into one map you can actually follow, from arithmetic to the 2024–26 frontier.

THE K.I.S.S LADDER
Every topic has four rungs, so you always know what "learning it" means:
• Information: you know it exists and what it is for
• Knowledge: you can teach it or hold a technical conversation about it
• Skill: you can build something real with it
• Success: others recognise, use or build on your work, whether that is SME status, a paper, a new technique, adoption, influence or income

Mark where you are on any topic with one tap, and watch your personal funnel grow.

243 TOPICS, CONNECTED
• Eight levels: arithmetic, core math, advanced math and computing, classical ML, deep learning, modern architectures, foundation models and LLMs, and the 2024–26 frontier
• An Applied AI section: robotics, autonomous driving, healthcare, drug discovery, climate, finance, search, document AI and more
• Every link is a real prerequisite, so you can trace exactly what a topic builds on and what it unlocks

FOR EVERY TOPIC
• A syllabus of subtopics to tick off
• Hand-picked courses, books, videos and key papers
• What each K.I.S.S rung means for that topic, including what success looks like
• Your own definition of success

LEARNING PATHS FOR 21 AI ROLES
Data Scientist, ML Engineer, AI Engineer, Forward Deployed Engineer, Research Scientist, MLOps, AI Safety, AI Product Manager and more. Each role has ordered phases with a teach-back, a capstone project and a milestone. Tell the app your background and it marks what you already know and where to start.

YOUR PROGRESS, YOUR WAY
• Works fully offline, no account needed
• Optional sign-in syncs your progress across phone and web
• Back up and restore at any time
• No ads, no tracking
